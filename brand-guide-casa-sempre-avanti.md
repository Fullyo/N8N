## 1. PROPERTY IDENTITY

**Official Brand Name:** Casa Sempre Avanti (also: Villas Sempre Avanti)
**Translation:** "Sempre Avanti" = *Always Forward* (Italian)
**Tagline:** *Hosted, Not Rented*
**Core Philosophy:** Guests are not checking into a rental — they are arriving at a fully hosted private estate. Every detail is personally attended to.

**The Estate:**
- **Villa Luisa** — 3 bedrooms / 3.5 bathrooms / private infinity pool / primary gathering villa
- **Villa Pietro** (also: Casa Pietro) — 2 bedrooms / 2.5 bathrooms / private infinity pool / more intimate and romantic
- **Together** = Casa Sempre Avanti / Villas Sempre Avanti — 5 bedrooms, 2 infinity pools, 250 feet of private beachfront

**Configuration flexibility:**
- Each villa can be rented independently
- Both together = full estate experience (ideal for groups 8–12)
- Social posts may target Villa Luisa, Villa Pietro, or the combined estate

## 2. LOCATION

**Address:** Patzcuaro Beach (exclusive private enclave), Sayulita, Nayarit, Mexico
**Region:** Riviera Nayarit / Mexican Pacific Coast

**Distances:**
- Sayulita village: 5 min by UTV / 8–10 min by car
- Punta de Mita: 25 min by UTV / short drive
- San Pancho: 15 min via Sayulita
- La Cruz de Huanacaxtle: 30 min by car (Sunday Market)
- Puerto Vallarta (PVR airport): ~55 min by car

**Setting:** Nestled between tropical rainforest/jungle and the Pacific Ocean. Beachfront access is direct — 65 steps from the infinity pool. Views of Monkey Mountain (Cerro del Mono), jungle, and open Pacific.

## 6. SIGNATURE EXPERIENCES (Post-worthy moments)

### Pizza Night
Ricardo fires the wood-burning oven (5-hour heat-up ritual). Guests choose toppings, watch their pizzas come out of the flames. Casual, interactive, always remembered. *Best post angle: anticipation, ritual, fire, family gathering*

### The Sunset Margarita
Every evening as the sun dips into the Pacific, handcrafted margaritas are served on the beach or terrace. A one-hour window where time slows down. Private bartender option available.
*Best post angle: golden hour, ocean, ritual, slow living*

### UTV Coastal Cruise
Polaris UTVs for exploring the coast from Punta de Mita to San Pancho at your own pace.
*Best post angle: freedom, adventure, coastal road, jungle*

### BBQ on the Beach
Fire, sand, ocean, and Ricardo's cooking directly on the shoreline.
*Best post angle: intimacy, fire on beach, group experience*

### Cook Your Catch
Guest catches fish via spearfishing or charter, Ricardo cooks it for dinner.
*Best post angle: from ocean to table, pure experience*

## 10. BRAND VOICE & TONE

### What the brand IS:
- Warm, intimate, personal — not cold luxury-hotel language
- Inviting and sensory — you can feel the ocean, smell the salt, taste the food
- Confident but not boastful — the experience speaks for itself
- Poetic but grounded — no fluff, no filler
- Story-driven — posts tell moments, not features

### Brand vocabulary (USE these words and phrases):
- "Hosted, not rented"
- "Fully hosted private estate"
- "Always Forward"
- "Five-star service on an intimate scale"
- "Secluded yet accessible"
- "Seamless"
- "Fire-lit"
- "Beachfront"
- "Sunrise to sunset"
- "Curated"
- "The rhythm of the ocean"
- "Riviera Nayarit"
- "Patzcuaro Beach"
- "Your team"
- "250 feet of beachfront"

### NEVER say in posts:
- "Private beach" — the beach is shared by the Patzcuaro Beach community (not exclusively owned)
- "All-inclusive" — food/drinks are not included in the room rate
- Pricing specifics in posts
- Markdown formatting (no ** bold, no #, no bullet points in captions)
- Hashtag blocks — limit to 3–5 relevant hashtags max
- Claims about availability or dates

### Tone by post type:
| Post type | Tone |
|---|---|
| Property features | Aspirational, calm, sensory |
| Activities | Energetic, vivid, adventurous |
| Food/Chef | Warm, intimate, mouthwatering |
| Wellness | Quiet, intentional, restorative |
| Events/Weddings | Emotional, celebratory, personal |
| Location/Sayulita | Local, authentic, curious |

## 14. CAPTION STRUCTURE GUIDELINES (for AI-generated posts)

- **Length:** 100–200 words
- **No markdown** (no bold, no hashtags mid-text, no bullet points)
- **Open with a scene or moment** — not "Welcome to..."
- **End with a soft call to action** — "Inquire to reserve your stay" or "Link in bio to book"
- **Include 3–5 relevant hashtags** at the end, separated from the body
- **Rotate perspectives** — sometimes write as the experience, sometimes as an invitation, sometimes as a story
