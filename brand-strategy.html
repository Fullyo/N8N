The 80/20 Content Rule

80% — Value & Inspiration (no direct ask)

Property showcase — beautiful spaces & details
Destination content — Sayulita, Baja, Riviera Nayarit
Lifestyle & aspiration — what a stay feels like
Behind the scenes — hosts, staff, local culture
Educational — why the region, why this property type

20% — Direct Ask (CTA)

Check availability — DM us or link in bio
Limited dates — create urgency around high season
Book direct — save vs. Airbnb/VRBO
Owner inquiries (LUX only) — DM for a free assessment
